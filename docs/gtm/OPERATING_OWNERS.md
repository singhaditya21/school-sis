# Interim GTM Operating Owners

> Effective operating default from 2026-08-21. This model keeps the sprint moving while the named product-readiness DRI is selected.

## Ownership model

| Workstream | Accountable owner | Execution DRI | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Market validation | Founder/CEO | Founder/CEO, with analyst support | Active on an interim basis |
| Product readiness | Founder/CEO until delegated | Senior-most engineer able to control deployment, data import, reconciliation, and security evidence | Name required within four working hours |

Accountability stays with one person. A team, function, agency, or committee may support the work but does not count as the owner.

## Market-validation owner

The Founder/CEO owns this workstream during founder-led validation because positioning, pricing, buyer access, and the decision to narrow or stop the wedge are founder decisions.

Required outcomes:

- Approve the first 20 Delhi NCR accounts and the first six interview candidates.
- Run or attend the first 15 buyer interviews.
- Enforce the interview evidence standard and record contradictory evidence.
- Approve proposals, concessions, and the decision to advance or reject the beachhead.
- Keep buyer discovery separate from feature-selling.

## Product-readiness DRI

Select the senior-most engineer who can make and defend a go/no-go decision across the pilot workflow—not necessarily the person with the largest team or most senior title.

The selected person must be able to:

1. Configure and secure the pilot demo environment.
2. Own the sample-data path and prevent sensitive-data leakage.
3. Demonstrate import validation, rerun safety, reconciliation, approvals, audit history, and exports.
4. Produce inspectable evidence for every readiness claim.
5. Stop a demo, proposal, or pilot when the evidence or security path is inadequate.

## Selection test

Choose the person who can answer “yes” to at least four of these five questions:

- Can they deploy or direct a production deployment without waiting for another function?
- Can they trace a fee record from source import through reconciliation and reporting?
- Can they decide whether a security or tenant-isolation issue blocks a pilot?
- Can they commit engineering capacity for the next two weeks?
- Will they personally sign the pilot-readiness evidence?

If nobody meets the test, the Founder/CEO remains the temporary accountable owner and names one engineering executor. The pilot scope then stays limited to synthetic data until readiness evidence exists.

## Daily cadence for the first 48 hours

| Timebox | Owner | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15 minutes, start of day | Founder + product DRI | Blockers, evidence due, and one decision per workstream |
| 30 minutes, mid-day | Product DRI | Demo/import/reconciliation evidence update |
| 30 minutes, end of day | Founder | Account, interview, and decision-log update |
