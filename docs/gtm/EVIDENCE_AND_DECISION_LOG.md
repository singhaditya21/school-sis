# Evidence and Decision Log

## Rules

1. Every strategic claim links to inspectable evidence.
2. A meeting, demo, or task does not count as proof by itself.
3. Record contradictory evidence; do not average it away.
4. Separate fact, participant claim, inference, assumption, and decision.
5. Do not store student records, payment identifiers, private contact details, or credentials here.
6. Revisit provisional decisions when their stated trigger occurs.
