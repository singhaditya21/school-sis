# ScholarMind GTM — First 48 Hours

> **Status:** Active execution pack
> **Started:** 2026-08-21
> **Launch geography:** Delhi NCR, confirmed 2026-08-21
> **Initial segment:** Private K-12 groups with 3–15 campuses and centralized finance or operations

## Objective

Make one commercial promise true and measurable: ScholarMind can give a multi-campus school group verified fee visibility, reconciliation, exception control, approval evidence, and executive reporting without requiring a big-bang ERP replacement.

The first 48 hours finish when the team has a controlled product scope, a target-account universe, consistent research instruments, a paid-pilot offer, accountable owners, and a schedule for the first six buyer interviews.

## Non-negotiable gate

No pricing, positioning, or roadmap conclusion becomes final merely because a meeting occurred. The finance-and-governance wedge advances only if at least five qualified school groups independently rank the same problem among their top three priorities and describe a current workaround, material consequence, and credible buying trigger.

No external outreach has been performed by this execution pack. Organization research uses public information; named contacts and warm paths must be supplied or approved before outreach.
