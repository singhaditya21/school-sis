# Group Finance Control Pilot

## Customer promise

In 8–12 weeks, give central leadership a reconciled view of fee performance, receivables, exceptions, approvals, and campus variance using existing source systems, before asking the group to replace its ERP.

## Ideal participant

- Private K-12 group with 3–15 campuses and approximately 2,000–20,000 students.
- Central CFO, COO, finance head, or MIS sponsor.
- Monthly consolidation depends on ERP exports and spreadsheets.
- Current pain includes delayed reconciliation, unclear receivables, approval leakage, or inconsistent campus reporting.
- Can provide authorized, minimally necessary source exports and operational SMEs.

## Scope

1. One or two pilot campuses.
2. One representative historical period plus current-period data.
3. Student, invoice, payment, waiver, refund, and settlement import.
4. Mapping, normalization, validation, and reconciliation.
5. Receivables, aging, exception, and variance views.
6. Waiver/refund approval flow and audit evidence.
7. Group and campus executive reporting.
8. Data-quality and implementation findings.
9. Final value review and group-rollout recommendation.

## Exclusions

- Full SIS replacement.
- Mobile parent-app rollout.
- Autonomous AI decisions or unreviewed predictions.
- Bespoke LMS, transport, library, HR, or classroom scope.
- Payment-provider switching unless explicitly contracted.
- Unbounded historical migration.
- Custom work that cannot be reused across the design-partner cohort.

## Commercial structure

- Provisional paid-pilot band: ₹2–4 lakh plus applicable taxes.
- Final quote follows the data workshop and source assessment.
- Pilot fee may be credited toward an annual group agreement signed within the proposal validity period.
- Third-party fees, unusual data remediation, travel, and custom integration are separately scoped.
- No free customization; any concession trades for a narrower scope, faster data access, reference rights, or annual commitment.

## Customer prerequisites

- Named executive sponsor and operational owner.
- Approved data-processing and security path.
- Authorized sample exports before final scope.
- Weekly working session and access to finance/ERP SMEs.
- Written baseline and success criteria.

Use [`PILOT_DATA_REQUEST.md`](./PILOT_DATA_REQUEST.md) to establish the minimum fields, secure-transfer path, data manifest, and go/no-go record.

## Success metrics

| Metric | Pilot target |
| --- | ---: |
| Time from usable data to first accepted output | ≤30 days |
| Financial records reconciled without unexplained variance | ≥99.5% |
| Critical security, tenant-isolation, or financial-control incidents | 0 |
| Sensitive pilot actions represented in audit history | 100% |
| Executive sponsor reviews completed | Weekly |
| Annual decision | Within 30 days of final value review |

Targets are provisional until the first three pilots establish a baseline.

## Proposal gate

Do not issue a proposal until a qualified sponsor has completed discovery and a data workshop has confirmed source availability, scope, security path, baseline, and economic outcome.
